LOAN POLICY

Document Type: Loan Policy
Document ID: LOAN-001
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

==================================================
1. OVERVIEW
==================================================

The bank provides loans to eligible customers subject to credit assessment,
product-specific requirements, documentation, and applicable lending
criteria.

Loan products may include:

- Personal loans
- Home loans
- Vehicle loans
- Education loans
- Other approved lending products

Specific terms differ by product.

==================================================
2. ELIGIBILITY
==================================================

Loan eligibility may depend on:

- Age
- Income
- Employment status
- Length of employment
- Credit history
- Existing liabilities
- Repayment capacity
- Debt-to-income ratio
- Requested loan amount
- Loan purpose
- Collateral, where applicable

Meeting minimum eligibility criteria does not guarantee loan approval.

==================================================
3. APPLICATION
==================================================

A customer may apply through an approved branch, digital channel, or other
application channel supported by the bank.

The application must contain accurate and complete information.

Providing false or misleading information may result in rejection or other
action under the loan agreement.

==================================================
4. DOCUMENTS
==================================================

Depending on the loan type, the bank may request:

- Identity proof
- Address proof
- Income proof
- Salary slips
- Bank statements
- Tax documents
- Employment documents
- Business documents
- Property documents
- Vehicle documents
- Educational documents
- Other supporting information

Additional documents may be requested during underwriting.

==================================================
5. CREDIT ASSESSMENT
==================================================

The bank assesses an applicant's ability and willingness to repay.

Factors may include:

- Credit history
- Existing loans
- Repayment history
- Income stability
- Existing monthly obligations
- Requested loan amount
- Loan tenure

Credit assessment does not rely solely on a single factor.

==================================================
6. LOAN AMOUNT
==================================================

The approved loan amount depends on the applicant's eligibility and the
specific product.

The requested amount may be reduced if the bank determines that the requested
amount exceeds the customer's repayment capacity.

==================================================
7. INTEREST RATE
==================================================

Loans may have fixed or variable interest rates.

A fixed-rate loan generally maintains the applicable rate according to the
loan agreement for the specified period.

A variable-rate loan may change based on the applicable benchmark and
contractual terms.

The applicable interest rate will be specified in the loan documentation.

==================================================
8. LOAN TENURE
==================================================

Loan tenure depends on the product and approved terms.

A longer tenure may reduce the scheduled installment but may increase the
total interest paid over the life of the loan.

A shorter tenure may result in higher periodic installments but may reduce
the overall interest cost.

==================================================
9. EMI
==================================================

For loans using an equated installment structure, the periodic installment
may include principal and interest components.

The installment amount depends on:

- Principal
- Interest rate
- Tenure
- Repayment structure

Customers should refer to the repayment schedule provided with the loan.

==================================================
10. REPAYMENT
==================================================

Repayments must be made according to the repayment schedule.

Customers are responsible for ensuring that sufficient funds are available
for automatic repayment where such a facility is used.

==================================================
11. MISSED PAYMENTS
==================================================

Failure to make a scheduled payment by the due date may result in:

- Late-payment charges
- Additional interest or applicable charges
- Collection activity
- Negative impact on credit history

Repeated missed payments may lead to default.

==================================================
12. DEFAULT
==================================================

A loan may be classified as being in default according to the applicable
loan agreement and regulatory requirements.

The bank may take recovery action permitted under the agreement and applicable
law.

For secured loans, the bank may have rights relating to the pledged or
mortgaged asset subject to applicable law and contractual terms.

==================================================
13. PREPAYMENT
==================================================

Customers may request partial or full prepayment.

Prepayment terms depend on the product, loan agreement, and applicable
regulations.

A prepayment charge may apply to certain loan products or circumstances.

Customers should request a foreclosure or prepayment statement before making
a large payment intended to close the loan.

==================================================
14. PART-PREPAYMENT
==================================================

A customer may request to make a partial prepayment where permitted.

Depending on the product, the customer may be able to:

- Reduce the outstanding principal
- Reduce the loan tenure
- Reduce the periodic installment

The available option depends on the loan agreement and bank procedures.

==================================================
15. LOAN CLOSURE
==================================================

A loan is closed after all required amounts have been paid and the bank has
completed the applicable closure process.

For secured loans, release of security or related documents may require
additional processing.

==================================================
16. LOAN STATEMENT
==================================================

Customers may request a loan statement showing information such as:

- Outstanding principal
- Interest charged
- Payments received
- Upcoming installments
- Remaining tenure
- Applicable charges

==================================================
17. CHANGE IN CUSTOMER INFORMATION
==================================================

Borrowers should notify the bank when there is a material change in contact
information, employment, or other information required under the loan
agreement.

==================================================
18. POLICY CHANGES
==================================================

The bank may update lending criteria, interest rates, fees, documentation
requirements, and product conditions subject to applicable agreements and
regulatory requirements.
