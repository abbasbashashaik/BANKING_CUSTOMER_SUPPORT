SAVINGS ACCOUNT POLICY

Document Type: Savings Account Policy
Document ID: SAV-001
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Last Reviewed: January 1, 2026

==================================================
1. OVERVIEW
==================================================

A savings account is a deposit account designed for customers who want to
deposit, hold, withdraw, and transfer money while earning interest on eligible
balances.

The bank offers several savings account variants. Each product may have
different minimum balance requirements, transaction limits, interest rates,
and fees.

The terms in this document apply to the Standard Savings Account unless
otherwise stated.

==================================================
2. ELIGIBILITY
==================================================

An individual who is at least 18 years old may apply for a Standard Savings
Account, subject to the bank's account-opening requirements.

Joint accounts may be opened subject to the bank's permitted operating
instructions.

Minor accounts may be opened under a guardian arrangement where permitted.

The bank may decline an application if required information or documentation
is not provided or if the application does not satisfy applicable
requirements.

==================================================
3. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
==================================================

Customers may be required to provide:

- Government-issued identity proof
- Address proof
- Recent photograph, where applicable
- Tax identification information, where applicable
- Mobile number
- Email address, where applicable
- Additional documents required for regulatory or risk purposes

The bank may request updated documents periodically.

==================================================
4. MINIMUM BALANCE
==================================================

The Standard Savings Account requires an average monthly balance of
INR 5,000.

The average monthly balance is calculated according to the bank's applicable
calculation method.

If the required balance is not maintained, a non-maintenance charge may
apply.

Customers should refer to the current Schedule of Charges for the applicable
fee.

Some account variants may have zero-balance or lower-balance requirements.

==================================================
5. ACCOUNT OPENING
==================================================

Accounts may be opened through supported branches or approved digital
account-opening channels.

Digital account opening may require identity verification, mobile
authentication, video verification, or other verification procedures.

The account becomes operational only after all required verification steps
are successfully completed.

==================================================
6. INTEREST
==================================================

Interest is calculated based on the eligible daily closing balance.

Interest is credited to the account at the frequency specified for the
applicable savings product.

Interest rates may change from time to time.

The applicable interest rate is determined by the bank's current rate schedule.

Customers should not assume that the interest rate at account opening will
remain unchanged throughout the life of the account.

==================================================
7. CASH DEPOSITS
==================================================

Customers may deposit cash through supported branches, cash deposit machines,
or other approved channels.

Cash transactions may be subject to transaction limits, verification
requirements, and applicable charges.

Large or unusual transactions may require additional information or
documentation.

==================================================
8. CASH WITHDRAWALS
==================================================

Customers may withdraw funds through branches, ATMs, or other approved
channels.

Withdrawals are subject to available account balance, transaction limits,
security checks, and applicable restrictions.

ATM withdrawals may have daily limits.

Branch withdrawals may require identity verification.

==================================================
9. ATM TRANSACTIONS
==================================================

Customers may use their eligible debit card at supported ATMs.

ATM transactions may include:

- Cash withdrawal
- Balance inquiry
- PIN change
- Mini statement
- Other services supported by the ATM

Free transaction limits may apply.

Transactions beyond the permitted free limit may attract charges.

==================================================
10. DEBIT CARD
==================================================

Eligible savings account customers may receive a debit card.

The card may be used for:

- ATM withdrawals
- Point-of-sale purchases
- Online purchases
- Contactless payments where supported

The customer must protect the card and authentication credentials.

Customers must not share their PIN or other confidential authentication
information.

==================================================
11. LOST OR STOLEN DEBIT CARD
==================================================

A lost or stolen debit card should be reported immediately.

The bank may block the card after receiving the request.

The customer may request a replacement card.

Replacement fees may apply depending on the circumstances and current
Schedule of Charges.

Customers should review recent transactions after losing a card and report
any unauthorized activity immediately.

==================================================
12. DIGITAL BANKING
==================================================

Customers may access their accounts through supported online and mobile
banking services.

Digital banking may allow customers to:

- View balances
- View transactions
- Transfer funds
- Download statements
- Manage beneficiaries
- Manage cards
- Update selected contact information
- Make eligible bill payments

Availability of individual features may vary.

==================================================
13. FUND TRANSFERS
==================================================

Customers may transfer money using supported domestic payment systems.

Transfer methods may have different:

- Processing times
- Transaction limits
- Fees
- Availability windows

A transfer may be delayed because of technical issues, beneficiary problems,
bank holidays, compliance checks, or incorrect transaction information.

==================================================
14. BENEFICIARIES
==================================================

Customers may add beneficiaries through supported banking channels.

Additional authentication may be required before a beneficiary becomes
active.

Some beneficiary additions may be subject to a cooling-off period.

Customers should verify beneficiary details before initiating a transfer.

==================================================
15. ACCOUNT STATEMENTS
==================================================

Customers may obtain statements through digital banking, ATMs, branches, or
other supported channels.

Statements may include:

- Transaction date
- Transaction description
- Debit amount
- Credit amount
- Running balance

Electronic statements may be free while physical or duplicate statements
may attract a fee.

==================================================
16. TRANSACTION DISPUTES
==================================================

Customers should report unauthorized or disputed transactions as soon as
possible.

The bank may request:

- Transaction details
- Account information
- Customer identification
- Supporting documents
- A written dispute declaration

The bank will investigate the dispute according to its applicable process.

The outcome of an investigation depends on transaction details and available
evidence.

==================================================
17. NOMINATION
==================================================

Eligible customers may register a nominee for their account according to
applicable procedures.

Customers should review nominee information periodically and update it when
necessary.

==================================================
18. ACCOUNT INACTIVITY
==================================================

An account with no qualifying customer-initiated activity for an extended
period may be classified as inactive or dormant according to applicable
rules.

Customers may be required to complete additional verification before
reactivating such an account.

==================================================
19. ACCOUNT CLOSURE
==================================================

Customers may request account closure through an approved channel.

Before closure:

- Outstanding charges must generally be settled.
- Pending transactions should be resolved.
- Any linked services may need to be cancelled.
- Remaining balance must be transferred or withdrawn according to the bank's
  closure process.

==================================================
20. CONTACT INFORMATION
==================================================

Customers should keep their registered mobile number, email address, and
address up to date.

Incorrect contact information may prevent important notifications from
reaching the customer.

==================================================
21. POLICY CHANGES
==================================================

The bank may modify product terms, interest rates, limits, and charges.
Changes will be communicated through applicable customer communication
channels where required.

The latest applicable terms take precedence over older versions.
