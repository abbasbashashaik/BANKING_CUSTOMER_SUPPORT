CREDIT CARD POLICY

Document Type: Credit Card Policy
Document ID: CARD-001
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

==================================================
1. OVERVIEW
==================================================

A credit card provides access to a revolving credit facility up to an
approved credit limit.

Use of the card is subject to the cardholder agreement, applicable fees,
credit limit, transaction restrictions, and security requirements.

==================================================
2. ELIGIBILITY
==================================================

Applicants must satisfy the bank's applicable eligibility requirements.

Eligibility may depend on:

- Age
- Income
- Credit history
- Employment
- Existing obligations
- Internal risk criteria

Approval is subject to the bank's credit assessment.

==================================================
3. CREDIT LIMIT
==================================================

Each approved cardholder receives a credit limit.

The available credit decreases when transactions are posted and generally
increases after eligible payments are credited.

The bank may periodically review the credit limit.

==================================================
4. AVAILABLE CREDIT
==================================================

Available credit is generally calculated based on the approved limit less
applicable outstanding amounts and pending transactions.

A transaction may be declined if it exceeds available credit or is blocked
for security or other reasons.

==================================================
5. BILLING CYCLE
==================================================

Transactions are grouped into billing cycles.

At the end of each billing cycle, a statement is generated.

The statement may include:

- Total amount due
- Minimum amount due
- Payment due date
- Transaction history
- Fees
- Interest
- Credits and payments

==================================================
6. PAYMENT DUE DATE
==================================================

Customers must make at least the required minimum payment by the due date.

Paying the total amount due may help avoid applicable interest on eligible
purchases subject to the card agreement.

==================================================
7. MINIMUM PAYMENT
==================================================

The minimum payment is the minimum amount the customer must pay by the due
date to keep the account current.

Paying only the minimum amount may result in interest being charged on the
remaining balance.

==================================================
8. INTEREST
==================================================

Interest may apply to unpaid balances according to the cardholder agreement.

Different transaction types may be treated differently.

For example, cash advances may have separate interest and fee rules.

==================================================
9. CASH ADVANCE
==================================================

Eligible cardholders may withdraw cash using supported channels.

Cash advances may attract:

- Cash advance fees
- Interest from the applicable transaction date
- ATM charges

Cash advances may not receive the same interest-free treatment as eligible
purchase transactions.

==================================================
10. FEES
==================================================

Potential credit card fees include:

- Annual fee
- Replacement card fee
- Late payment fee
- Cash advance fee
- Foreign transaction fee
- Over-limit fee, where applicable
- Other service charges

The actual applicable fee depends on the card product and current
Schedule of Charges.

==================================================
11. OVERDUE PAYMENTS
==================================================

A payment that is not received by the due date may result in:

- Late payment charges
- Additional interest
- Suspension of certain card privileges
- Negative credit reporting where applicable

Repeated overdue payments may result in further action.

==================================================
12. LOST OR STOLEN CARD
==================================================

Customers must immediately report a lost or stolen card.

The bank may block the card to prevent further transactions.

A replacement card may be issued after verification.

Customers should review transactions and report unauthorized transactions
promptly.

==================================================
13. UNAUTHORIZED TRANSACTIONS
==================================================

Customers should immediately notify the bank when they identify a transaction
they did not authorize.

The bank may temporarily block the card and investigate the transaction.

Customers may be required to provide additional information or complete a
dispute form.

==================================================
14. CARD SECURITY
==================================================

Customers must:

- Keep the physical card secure.
- Keep the PIN confidential.
- Never share OTPs or authentication codes.
- Avoid entering card details on suspicious websites.
- Report suspected fraud immediately.

Bank employees will not normally require customers to disclose confidential
authentication credentials.

==================================================
15. CONTACTLESS PAYMENTS
==================================================

Contactless payments may be available on eligible cards.

Certain transaction values may require additional authentication according
to applicable payment-network rules.

==================================================
16. INTERNATIONAL TRANSACTIONS
==================================================

International transactions may be subject to:

- Foreign exchange conversion
- Foreign transaction fees
- Applicable taxes
- Merchant or network charges

The final amount may differ from the original foreign-currency amount due
to exchange-rate conversion.

==================================================
17. CARD CANCELLATION
==================================================

A cardholder may request card cancellation.

Outstanding balances and applicable charges remain payable after cancellation.

Cancellation of the card does not automatically cancel an outstanding debt.

==================================================
18. CREDIT LIMIT REVIEW
==================================================

The bank may review a customer's credit limit based on payment behavior,
income information, account history, and internal criteria.

An increase is not guaranteed.

==================================================
19. DISPUTED TRANSACTIONS
==================================================

Customers should report disputed transactions as soon as possible.

The bank may investigate merchant information, transaction records,
authentication details, and other available evidence.

==================================================
20. POLICY CHANGES
==================================================

The bank may modify credit card fees, limits, features, and terms subject to
the cardholder agreement and applicable requirements.
