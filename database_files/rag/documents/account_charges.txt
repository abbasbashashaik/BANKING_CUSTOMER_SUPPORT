ACCOUNT CHARGES

Document Type: Account Charges and Fees
Document ID: FEE-001
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

==================================================
1. PURPOSE
==================================================

This document describes common charges that may apply to banking products.

Actual charges depend on the customer's product, transaction type, channel,
account status, and applicable Schedule of Charges.

==================================================
2. SAVINGS ACCOUNT CHARGES
==================================================

Possible charges include:

- Minimum balance non-maintenance fee
- Physical statement fee
- Duplicate statement fee
- Cheque book fee
- Debit card replacement fee
- Certain cash transaction fees
- Certain transfer fees

==================================================
3. ATM CHARGES
==================================================

Customers may receive a specified number of free ATM transactions depending
on their account and applicable rules.

Transactions beyond the applicable free limit may incur charges.

Different charges may apply for:

- Cash withdrawals
- Balance inquiries
- Other ATM services

==================================================
4. DEBIT CARD FEES
==================================================

Possible debit card charges include:

- Card issuance fee
- Annual fee
- Replacement fee
- International transaction charges
- Certain ATM charges

Fees vary by card type.

==================================================
5. FUND TRANSFER FEES
==================================================

Transfer fees may depend on:

- Transfer method
- Amount
- Destination
- Transaction type
- Domestic or international status
- Channel used

Some transfers may be free while others may incur charges.

==================================================
6. FAILED TRANSACTIONS
==================================================

A failed or returned transaction may attract a fee where permitted by the
applicable product terms.

Examples include certain failed payment instructions or returned payment
transactions.

==================================================
7. LOAN FEES
==================================================

Loan-related fees may include:

- Processing fee
- Documentation fee
- Prepayment charge where applicable
- Late-payment charge
- Statement fee
- Other product-specific fees

==================================================
8. CREDIT CARD FEES
==================================================

Credit card charges may include:

- Annual fee
- Late payment fee
- Cash advance fee
- Foreign transaction fee
- Replacement card fee
- Other card-specific charges

==================================================
9. TAXES
==================================================

Applicable taxes and government levies may be charged in addition to banking
fees.

==================================================
10. FEE WAIVERS
==================================================

Certain customers or products may qualify for fee waivers.

A waiver may depend on:

- Account type
- Balance maintained
- Transaction volume
- Relationship with the bank
- Promotional terms

A fee waiver for one transaction does not necessarily apply to future
transactions.

==================================================
11. FEE DISPUTES
==================================================

Customers who believe a fee was incorrectly charged may contact the bank
through an approved service channel.

The customer should provide:

- Account information
- Transaction date
- Charge amount
- Description of the disputed fee

The bank may review the transaction and applicable terms.

==================================================
12. CHANGES TO FEES
==================================================

Fees may change over time.

Customers should refer to the latest applicable Schedule of Charges before
initiating a chargeable transaction.
