BANKING FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Document Type: Banking FAQ
Document ID: FAQ-001
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

==================================================
ACCOUNT OPENING
==================================================

Q: How do I open a savings account?

A: Complete the bank's account-opening process and provide the required
identity, address, and other requested information. Additional verification
may be required.

Q: What documents are needed to open an account?

A: Depending on the account and customer profile, identity proof, address
proof, photograph, tax information, mobile number, and other documents may
be required.

Q: Can I open a joint account?

A: Joint accounts may be available subject to the bank's account-opening
rules and permitted operating instructions.

Q: Can a minor open a savings account?

A: Minor accounts may be available under a guardian arrangement subject to
applicable requirements.

==================================================
BALANCE AND INTEREST
==================================================

Q: What is the minimum balance?

A: The Standard Savings Account has an average monthly balance requirement of
INR 5,000. Other account products may have different requirements.

Q: What happens if I do not maintain the minimum balance?

A: A non-maintenance charge may apply according to the applicable Schedule
of Charges.

Q: How is savings account interest calculated?

A: Interest is calculated using the eligible daily closing balance and is
credited according to the applicable product terms.

Q: Can the interest rate change?

A: Yes. Savings account interest rates may be changed by the bank.

==================================================
CASH AND ATM
==================================================

Q: How can I withdraw cash?

A: Cash may generally be withdrawn through eligible ATMs or branches, subject
to applicable limits and verification.

Q: Is there an ATM withdrawal limit?

A: Yes. ATM withdrawal limits may apply based on the card and account type.

Q: Are ATM transactions free?

A: Customers may receive a specified number of free transactions. Additional
transactions may incur applicable charges.

Q: What should I do if an ATM did not dispense cash but my account was
debited?

A: Report the transaction to the bank promptly with the ATM location,
transaction date, approximate time, and amount. The bank will investigate
the transaction.

==================================================
DEBIT CARD
==================================================

Q: What should I do if I lose my debit card?

A: Report the card as lost or stolen immediately so that the bank can block
the card and help arrange a replacement.

Q: Can I get a replacement debit card?

A: Replacement cards may be issued after verification. A replacement fee
may apply.

Q: Should I share my PIN with bank employees?

A: No. Customers should never disclose their PIN or other confidential
authentication credentials.

==================================================
TRANSFERS
==================================================

Q: How can I transfer money?

A: Customers may use supported electronic transfer channels, mobile banking,
online banking, branches, or other approved methods.

Q: Why is my transfer pending?

A: A transfer may remain pending because of processing delays, technical
issues, beneficiary activation requirements, compliance checks, incorrect
details, or other operational reasons.

Q: What happens if I enter the wrong beneficiary details?

A: Incorrect beneficiary information can cause a transaction to fail or,
depending on the circumstances, result in funds being sent to an unintended
recipient. Customers should verify beneficiary details before confirming
transactions.

Q: Can I cancel a transfer?

A: Cancellation depends on the transfer status and payment method. Contact
the bank immediately if a transfer needs to be stopped.

==================================================
LOANS
==================================================

Q: How do I apply for a loan?

A: Submit an application and the documents required for the relevant loan
product. The bank will assess eligibility and repayment capacity.

Q: Is loan approval guaranteed?

A: No. Approval depends on the bank's credit assessment and applicable
eligibility criteria.

Q: What affects loan eligibility?

A: Factors may include income, employment, credit history, existing debt,
repayment capacity, loan amount, tenure, and product-specific criteria.

Q: What happens if I miss an EMI?

A: A missed payment may result in applicable charges and may negatively
affect your credit history.

Q: Can I prepay my loan?

A: Partial or full prepayment may be available depending on the loan
agreement and applicable rules.

Q: Can I reduce my EMI after making a part-prepayment?

A: Depending on the loan product, a customer may be able to reduce the EMI
or reduce the remaining tenure. The available option depends on the loan
terms and bank process.

Q: How do I close my loan?

A: Request a foreclosure or closure statement, pay the required outstanding
amount, and complete the bank's loan-closure process.

==================================================
CREDIT CARDS
==================================================

Q: What is a credit limit?

A: A credit limit is the maximum outstanding credit facility available on
the card under the applicable terms.

Q: What is available credit?

A: Available credit is generally the unused portion of the approved credit
limit after considering applicable outstanding and pending transactions.

Q: What is the minimum amount due?

A: It is the minimum amount that must be paid by the payment due date to
keep the card account current.

Q: Is paying the minimum amount enough?

A: Paying the minimum amount may keep the account from becoming overdue,
but the remaining balance may continue to attract applicable interest and
charges.

Q: What happens if I miss a credit card payment?

A: Late payment charges and additional interest may apply, and repeated
overdue payments may affect credit history.

Q: What should I do if my credit card is stolen?

A: Contact the bank immediately to block the card and report any transactions
you do not recognize.

Q: Can I use my credit card internationally?

A: International use may be available subject to card settings, merchant
acceptance, transaction limits, foreign exchange conversion, and applicable
fees.

==================================================
FEES
==================================================

Q: Why was I charged a banking fee?

A: Fees may apply for services such as minimum balance non-maintenance,
ATM usage beyond applicable free limits, card replacement, statements,
transfers, or other services.

Q: Can a banking fee be reversed?

A: A fee may be reviewed if the customer believes it was incorrectly charged.
Any reversal or waiver is subject to the bank's applicable rules.

Q: Where can I find current fees?

A: Customers should refer to the latest applicable Schedule of Charges.

==================================================
SECURITY AND FRAUD
==================================================

Q: What should I do if I see an unauthorized transaction?

A: Contact the bank immediately through an approved channel and report the
transaction. The bank may block the relevant card or account access and
investigate the transaction.

Q: Should I share an OTP with anyone?

A: No. Customers should not share OTPs, PINs, passwords, CVVs, or other
authentication credentials with anyone.

Q: What if someone claiming to be from the bank asks for my password?

A: Do not disclose the password. End the communication and contact the bank
using an official customer-service channel.

Q: How can I protect myself from phishing?

A: Avoid suspicious links, verify the website address before entering
credentials, never disclose authentication codes, and contact the bank if
you suspect fraud.

==================================================
ACCOUNT STATEMENTS
==================================================

Q: How can I get my account statement?

A: Statements may be available through mobile banking, online banking,
branches, ATMs, or other supported channels.

Q: Can I get an old statement?

A: Historical statements may be available subject to the bank's record
retention and statement-request procedures. A fee may apply for some
physical or duplicate statements.

==================================================
ACCOUNT CLOSURE
==================================================

Q: How do I close my savings account?

A: Submit an account-closure request through an approved channel and settle
any applicable outstanding charges or transactions.

Q: Can I close an account with a negative balance?

A: Outstanding amounts generally need to be settled before the account can
be fully closed.

==================================================
COMPLAINTS
==================================================

Q: How do I file a banking complaint?

A: Customers should first contact the bank through an approved customer
service channel and provide sufficient details about the issue.

Q: What information should I include in a complaint?

A: Include the relevant account or transaction reference, date, amount,
description of the issue, and supporting information where appropriate.

Q: What if I am not satisfied with the response?

A: Customers may escalate the complaint through the bank's designated
grievance process and, where applicable, the relevant external dispute or
regulatory mechanism.

==================================================
GENERAL
==================================================

Q: Can bank policies change?

A: Yes. Product terms, interest rates, fees, limits, and procedures may
change subject to applicable agreements and requirements.

Q: Which document applies if two documents have different information?

A: The latest applicable product agreement, official schedule, or policy
normally takes precedence over an older document. Customers should contact
the bank for clarification when there is a conflict.

Q: Are these FAQ answers a substitute for the account agreement?

A: No. The applicable account, loan, or card agreement contains the
binding terms for the customer's specific product.
